Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Notification Engine collects, uses, and protects information when you use our platform. We are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly (account details, API credentials), information generated by using the service (notification payloads, delivery logs, provider attempt records), and standard usage data (IP addresses, request timestamps).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to deliver the notification service, provide delivery tracking, improve reliability, and respond to support requests. We do not sell your data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Providers

Notification payloads are transmitted to configured email and SMS providers to facilitate delivery. These providers receive only the data necessary to deliver the notification. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

5. Security

Provider credentials are stored securely and are never exposed through the notification API. API access requires JWT authentication. We implement industry-standard security practices.

6. Data Retention

Notification records and delivery logs are retained to support delivery tracking. Account data is retained for the duration of your account and deleted upon request after account closure.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes. See also our Terms & Conditions.
